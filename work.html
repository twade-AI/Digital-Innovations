Your work.
Keep it with you.

Lessons save evidence, rehearsals and notes in this browser. Signing in syncs AEP progress, but does not move all your work. Download a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data. Keep it somewhere private.

1. Take a copy

The backup includes course progress, notes, reflections, evidence, rehearsals, project answers and the capstone on this device. It excludes sign-in credentials. It is a readable JSON file, so treat it like your other schoolwork.

2. Open it on another device

Choose a backup to inspect. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Existing work is kept unless you explicitly choose to replace conflicting entries.

When this device has different work for the same entry

Completion and learning are different

Marking a lesson complete records that you finished it. Self-checks and examples help you improve, but do not award a teacher’s grade. Keep your first attempt, revision and independent answer as evidence of what you can explain.
