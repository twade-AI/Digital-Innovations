ℹ️ What's in Fluency — and what's not

Fluency is a 20-lesson subset curated from the 59-lesson full course. Same slides, same explanations — just a focused route for learners who want AI literacy without a formal syllabus.

Included:

  • How LLMs actually work · prompting · hallucinations · bias
  • Spotting deepfakes, misinformation and AI-generated content
  • Practical use: writing, research, and when not to use AI
  • A printable certificate when you finish all 20 lessons

Not included (available in the full course):

  • GCSE Computer Science exam prep and mark-scheme practice
  • Unit assessments, teacher-facing resources, gradebook
  • Deeper technical lessons (transformers internals, RL, training pipelines)
