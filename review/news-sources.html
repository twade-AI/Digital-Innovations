Selection rules

  • Choose from the previous 45 days and reject future-dated or undated entries. Prefer publication dates over later update dates.
  • Prioritise creative work, games, robotics, science and environmental questions. Reduce finance/deal and customer-marketing stories.
  • Take one story per publisher before repeats. Allow at most two per publisher and two maker/community posts in total.
  • Remove duplicate titles and URLs, including common tracking parameters. The same event can still appear through different perspectives.
  • Keep the previous feed if fewer than three stories from three sources survive a refresh.
  • Show source perspective and subscription notices. Each automatically selected story has a topic-based discussion question; this is not a claim that an editor read or fact-checked the full article.
  • Keep earlier coverage in an expandable archive instead of presenting it as current news. Teacher picks remain available.

The Verge endpoint was corrected. Google DeepMind's proposed endpoint did not return usable XML during checking, so the existing working Google AI feed is retained. No new paid service or API key is required.

Checks

RSS/Atom parsing, publication dates, safe URLs, deduplication, freshness, quotas and topic classification have automated tests. Desktop and phone browser checks cover the four-publisher homepage teaser, topic filters, discussion questions, archive and safe rendering of feed text.
