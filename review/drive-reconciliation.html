Teaching resources: source-to-site map

Reconciled 26 October 2026. The revised teaching pathway contains 37 lessons and project sessions, with 328 slides. Both pathways are retained at the course owner's request: the Climate Innovation project sits alongside the original Removes course.

Source choices

The newer foundation investigations take precedence over duplicate introductory decks: Inside Gemini and its Orbitball exercise, Why Am I Seeing This, How We Talk About AI, and Can AI Think. The seven October study lessons use their teaching slides, pupil activities, teacher explanations and the cool-roof, memory-lab and fictional lunch-queue source cards. The cards are included in the site so pupils can work without access to Drive.

The eight revised Climate Innovation decks supply the project sequence. The nine digital-productivity presentations and six digital-literacy project presentations supply the practical pathways. The festive advert is optional. Material has been adapted for independent work, rather than reproduced as a slide-file viewer. Original Drive files remain unchanged.

Adaptations for online study

  • Explicit explanations, scenarios, suggested answers and an unaided exit response in every lesson.
  • Solo routes for group work; self-review is distinguished from independent user testing.
  • Fictional spreadsheet datasets replace missing external worksheets. Practical tasks use practice files and restricted sharing.
  • NotebookLM claims now require citation checking. Image verification focuses on provenance and corroboration rather than unreliable visual tells.
  • App features depend on the pupil's approved account. Drawing, planning and manual evidence tables provide alternatives.
  • Distinct lesson IDs and storage keys preserve the original course's progress. “Save my work” downloads revised responses and notes locally as JSON; it is an archive, not a cross-device restore or teacher submission service.

Scope and limits

Duplicate PDF exports and restricted teacher assessment answers are not published. Existing Units 3–7 remain in the original course; their folder inventory is not represented as a fresh line-by-line review. No U8-04 or U8-05 resources were present in the supplied productivity set, so no replacements have been invented.

Completion records participation, not mastery or an externally verified qualification. App instructions require school-account testing before classroom rollout. Offline reading requires a successful initial cache installation; external research, videos and AI tools require connectivity.
