What changed

  • Removed 19 duplicate AEP decks. The shared lessons now use the same slides, activities, quizzes and evidence tasks as Removes.
  • Kept specialist retrieval, AI-detection, prompt-injection, copyright, reasoning, policy and capstone work. These develop additional skills.
  • Shared lessons open in the Removes player within the AEP sequence. Next lesson and back to course return to the correct pathway. Completion remains separate; notes and evidence are shared.
  • Earlier AEP notes, reflections and evidence remain available in the AEP portfolio, labelled as earlier work.
  • Corrected unsupported claims about employment, detector accuracy and privacy, and replaced live companion testing with a fictional design task. Corrections reach every pathway using those lessons.
