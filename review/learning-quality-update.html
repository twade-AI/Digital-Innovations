What pupils will notice

Every distinct lesson now has a short fictional case, a saved first judgement, a plausible mistake, a stronger worked response and a transfer prompt. The examples open after the pupil writes something, including uncertainty. This is guidance for self-review, not an automated judgement of answer quality.

There are 94 bespoke cases: 40 Removes, 40 specialist AEP and 14 project sessions. Passport and the 19 shared AEP lessons use the actual Removes cases and saved work. Removes stays at 40 lessons and Passport at 20. The eight Climate Innovation and six Digital Literacy sessions stay separate.

The approved cream, green, blue and coral design remains, with illustrated explanations, coloured comparison panels and clearer controls. No new course slots have been added.

More careful teaching

Seven older Removes lessons have complete revised explanations, activities, scenarios, quizzes, summaries and matching diagrams: deepfakes (119), misinformation (121), fact-checking (122), bias (125), jobs (126), companions (131) and agents (134). Historical studies are dated and conclusions bounded; simulations are labelled. Earlier notes are preserved separately from the new revision.

Each project session has a curated source pack, an original offline briefing and a worked source comparison. All nine distinct project source URLs resolved successfully on the review date. Existing videos remain supplementary; the core teaching and tasks do not depend on watching them. A full caption, age-suitability and external-media review is still outstanding.

Easier to find your way

Learn resumes the chosen course and an unfinished lesson. Course switching and search cover all pathways. AEP section URLs survive reload and browser Back. Passport lesson and evidence links open the requested content. Project Continue stays inside the chosen pathway.

Reading preferences carry across pathways. Lessons focus their heading when opened, make the background inactive, retain keyboard focus inside the dialog and announce slide changes. Page structure, visible focus, link styling, contrast and touch controls have been improved.

Keep work and news dependable

Save or move my work downloads local work without authentication tokens and restores it on another browser. Import first previews conflicts; existing entries are kept by default. Choosing replacement downloads a safety copy first. Quota failure rollback is tested. Signing in still does not synchronise every kind of work.

The successful live news fetch produced 12 stories from 10 publishers. The site now shows snapshot freshness and warns when it is older than 48 hours or offline. Insufficient feed diversity fails the refresh instead of silently reporting success; workflow failures are visible in Actions. The existing daily schedule is 06:17 UTC. The next deployed scheduled run must still be checked through publication.
