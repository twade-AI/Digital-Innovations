The newer teaching now replaces content inside the established Removes sequence. There is no second foundations, study or tools course to choose between.

  • 40 lessons in seven units. All existing lesson IDs and their order remain stable. Nineteen lesson slots have replacement teaching: thirteen foundations/study lessons and six practical lessons.
  • 20 Passport lessons. They use the same Removes content, titles and evidence tasks, including the updated teaching.
  • Two existing project pathways. Climate Innovation remains eight sessions and Digital Literacy remains six. They sit on the separate projects page; they do not extend the Removes completion total.
  • Digital tools taught in context. The nine former tools sessions and optional advert brief are folded into relevant Removes lessons. The advert is a choice inside Creativity.
  • Evidence in every lesson. The existing forty creative briefs remain, with checked practice examples and independent routes in the replacement teaching.

Saved work and links

Existing Removes and Passport completion records remain. Replacement slide notes and exit answers use a new content revision, shared consistently between the two players. Earlier notes and exit answers remain visible with an earlier-version label in the portfolio. Evidence tasks keep their existing IDs and saved responses. The seven end-of-unit assessment routes remain available.

Old links to the 23 integrated revised sessions open their matching Removes lesson. Project progress migrates only the fourteen project IDs into a new progress key, preserving the original revised progress record. The projects page can download project work and earlier revised responses, including material from the retired duplicate lessons. A completed older lesson is not a new assessment of the replacement content.

Scope and checks

This is a curriculum consolidation, not a claim that every retained lesson has had a new source audit. Retained lessons keep the earlier content corrections. Automated checks cover the exact 40/20 counts, nineteen replacements, all 23 redirects, evidence, unit assessments, revised slide visuals and the standalone Passport export. Browser results are recorded in the validation file.
